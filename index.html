A Healthy Lifestyle

Being healthy is not about one big change. It is about a few small habits repeated every single day. Eat fresh food, move your body, drink enough water and sleep on time. Do this for a month and you will feel the difference.

The five habits that matter most:

  • Eat home-cooked, balanced meals
  • Exercise for at least 30 minutes a day
  • Drink 8 glasses of water
  • Sleep 7 to 8 hours every night
  • Avoid smoking, alcohol and junk food

Healthy Food

Your plate decides your energy. Fill half of it with fruits and vegetables.

🍎 Fruits

Apples, bananas, oranges and papaya give you vitamins, fibre and natural sugar instead of the processed kind.

🥦 Vegetables

Spinach, carrot, broccoli and beans are full of iron and minerals. Eat them in different colours every day.

🥚 Protein

Dal, eggs, paneer, milk and nuts repair your muscles and keep you full for longer so you snack less.

🌾 Whole Grains

Brown rice, oats, ragi and whole wheat release energy slowly, so you stay active the whole day.

Exercise

You do not need a gym membership to stay fit. Walking, cycling, skipping and simple home workouts are enough to keep your heart strong.

🚶 Walking

A brisk 30 minute walk burns calories, lowers blood pressure and clears your head.

🧘 Yoga

Improves flexibility and breathing, and is one of the best ways to reduce stress.

🏃 Running

Builds stamina fast. Start with 10 minutes and increase a little every week.

🏋️ Strength

Push ups, squats and planks at home build muscle and protect your bones.

Sleep & Water Tips

😴 Sleep Tips

  • Sleep and wake up at the same time daily
  • Switch off your phone 30 minutes before bed
  • Keep the room dark, quiet and cool
  • No coffee or tea after 6 pm
  • Aim for 7 to 8 hours, not 4 or 5

💧 Water Tips

  • Start the morning with one glass of water
  • Carry a bottle to college and refill it
  • Drink water before meals, not just after
  • Dark yellow urine means you need more water
  • Drink extra on hot days and after exercise

Health Awareness Information

Most serious diseases can be controlled if they are found early. Awareness is the first step of prevention.

🩺 Yearly Check-up

Get your blood pressure, sugar and haemoglobin checked once a year, even if you feel perfectly fine.

🧠 Mental Health

Stress and anxiety are health problems too. Talk to someone you trust and ask for help early.

🚭 Say No

Tobacco and alcohol damage the lungs, liver and heart. The best day to stop is today.

🧼 Hygiene

Washing hands with soap for 20 seconds prevents a large share of common infections.
